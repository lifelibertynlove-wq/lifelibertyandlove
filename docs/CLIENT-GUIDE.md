# How to update your website — a simple guide

Your website has a private editing panel. You never need to touch code, and you can't break anything: every change is saved safely and can be undone.

---

## Publishing a new blog post

1. Go to **lifelibertyandlove.com/admin/** and log in with your email and password.
2. Click **Blog posts** in the left menu, then the blue **New Blog post** button.
3. Fill in the fields:
   - **Title** — the headline of your post.
   - **Subtitle** — the short line that appears under the title (optional).
   - **Short description** — 1–2 sentences. This is what Google and Facebook show when your post is shared.
   - **Publish date** — usually today.
   - **Category** — pick one: Christian Thought, Relationships, Responding to Culture, or Struggles & Overcoming.
   - **Featured image** — click *Choose an image* and upload a photo from your computer. This is the big picture at the top of the post.
   - **Post content** — write here just like in a Word document. The toolbar lets you make text **bold**, *italic*, add headings, quotes, links, and insert pictures inside the article.
4. When you're happy, click **Publish → Publish now** (top right).
5. That's it! Your post appears on the website within about one minute.

## Saving a draft (not ready to publish yet)

Instead of Publish, click **Save**. The post is kept as a **Draft** — visible only to you in the panel. When you're ready, open it and click **Publish**.

## Editing an existing post or fixing a typo

1. Log in at **/admin/**, click **Blog posts**.
2. Click the post you want to change, edit it, and click **Publish** again.

## Adding a picture inside an article

1. Place your cursor where you want the picture.
2. In the toolbar of the content box, click the **+** (or image icon) → **Image** → *Choose an image* → upload.

## Deleting a post

Open the post in the panel and click **Delete entry** (top right). It disappears from the website in about a minute.

## Good to know

- **Changes take ~1 minute** to appear on the live site. Refresh the page if you don't see them right away.
- **Photos**: pictures around 1200–1600 px wide look best. Very large phone photos work too — the site optimizes what visitors download.
- **Messages from readers**: the contact form and new subscriber sign-ups arrive in your email automatically.
- **Comments** on posts are managed automatically; you'll be notified of new ones.
- If you ever get stuck, nothing is ever lost — every version of every post is saved. Just reach out and it can be restored.
